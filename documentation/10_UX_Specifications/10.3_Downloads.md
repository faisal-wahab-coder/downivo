# 10.3 Downloads

**Route:** `/downloads`  
**Widget:** `downloads_screen.dart`

## Purpose

Operational queue — the heart of the app.

## App bar

Title Downloads. Actions: search, pause all, resume all, history. FAB: add URL.

## Body groups

1. Downloading
2. Queued (reorderable drag handle)
3. Paused
4. Completed (collapsible)
5. Failed (collapsible)

Filters: All / Active / Completed / Failed.

## Tile (`_DownloadTile`)

Title (or fileName), platform/type, `DownloadProgressDetails` (bytes, speed, ETA), status chip, `DownloadTrailingActions` (pause/resume/cancel/retry/share/save to gallery for photos and videos). Tap a completed tile to open the file. If Files deleted that file, show **Removed from Files** and hide share/gallery.

Include preparing and verifying in copy/chips.

## Empty

`EmptyState` + Add URL CTA.

## Errors

Snackbar on enqueue failure. No dedicated error route.
