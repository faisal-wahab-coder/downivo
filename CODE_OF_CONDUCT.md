# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Using welcoming and inclusive language

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, email, real name, or other identifying data) without explicit permission
- Sharing secrets, credentials, or exploit details in public channels
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and related channels) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report incidents through **GitHub**:

1. For public, non-sensitive conduct issues: open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/faisal-wahab-coder/downivo/issues) with the facts, without extra personal data.
2. For harassment or safety concerns: use a **[private GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/faisal-wahab-coder/downivo/security/advisories/new)** or contact a maintainer through GitHub if available.

**Repository owner:** add a dedicated contact method here before public launch if you do not want reports to go through GitHub only.

```text
Conduct contact (configure before public release):
  GitHub Security Advisories, or a maintainer address you publish here
```

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these community impact guidelines:

1. **Correction** — private warning; public apology may be requested
2. **Warning** — interaction limits for a period of time
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the community for a specified period
4. **Permanent ban** — no remaining community interaction
